About us

Who are Sparks Auto Electric?

Sparks Auto Electrics, located at 9 Maunsell Street, has since expanded and continued to serve the vehicle owners of Christchurch.

Our customers range from the domestic car owner, to the leisure craft enthusiast (both marine and camping), to the large commercial trucking fleet operators. Whatever your needs in vehicle electrical systems, we can help you.

Here at Sparks our busy team of skilled and qualified professionals repair a huge range of faults on a vast variety of vehicles. Cars, trucks, commercial and agricultural machinery, forklifts, boats, caravans and motor homes are all in a day’s work. Mobile and after-hours call-out services are also available.

What can I expect when I bring my vehicle in?

Park in our car lot and talk to our friendly, helpful staff to make sure your problems are quickly and accurately attended to. With specialised equipment and knowledge, our friendly team will discuss and explain technical things in simple, easy-to-understand language.

We will give you a variety of repair options and keep you informed as work progresses. Our workshop uses the latest test and diagnostic equipment to reduce the cost of your repairs.
